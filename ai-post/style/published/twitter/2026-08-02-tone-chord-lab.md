**Tweet 1 (Hook):**

**Tweet 2 (Feature):**

🎸 Chords, not just notes
It hears the whole voicing and names the chord — root plus every note, matched against 10 templates across all 12 keys. Pluck a C major and it says C major, not one note that happened to win the fight.

---

**Tweet 3 (Feature):**

🎵 Built for bends, useful everywhere
A harmonica bend is a continuous slide most tuners chase and lag behind. Mine fuses two detectors — YIN in the time domain plus harmonic-spectrum scoring — so bends track live and rich overtones don't fool it.
